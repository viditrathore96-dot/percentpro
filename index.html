Why use a percentage calculator?

Percentage calculations appear in exam results, discounts, marks, averages, growth and everyday decisions. PercentPro is built to make these calculations quick without requiring an account or installing an app.

Common percentage formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

For example, if you score 72 out of 80, your percentage is (72 ÷ 80) × 100 = 90%.
